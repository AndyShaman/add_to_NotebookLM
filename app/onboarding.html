Add to NotebookLM

Thanks for installing! Here is what the extension can do.

✨ What it does
  • 📔 Add any page or YouTube video to Gemini Notebook (NotebookLM) in one click.
  • 📋 Bulk import of links, browser tabs, playlists and entire channels.
  • 💬 Import YouTube comments for audience analysis.
  • 🗑️ Bulk delete sources in a notebook.
🚀 Quick start
Pin the extension to the toolbar, open any page and click the icon.
